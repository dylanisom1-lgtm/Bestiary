    Moluth

    Living reliquaries of another age, another time.

    The shell of the mature Moluth — barnacled and ringed — resembles those spiralling tidal stones as found within the Sea. Yet beneath their pelagic exterior there lies not one singular creature, but an entire chorus of strange, amorphous life: a zooidic colony of impossible, protean things.

    Behaviour & Impact

    Sentience is determined by appendage. Baseline is animal-level instinct; a Crown of Polyps grants human-like intellect, overriding all other behavioural impulses. The number of appendages is set by size, and appendages stack.

    Their appendages — feelers, limbs, mouths, eyes, and organs stranger still — act of their own accord. Some delicately survey the world; others whisper in a thousand inhuman tongues; others still drip with noxious ichor, unfurling tendrils tipped in human hands.

    Kinds Unto Themselves

    Pseudo Gods

    There are beings in this world which fit no taxonomy. They are not species, for they hail from no lineage. They are one-off castaways, carved from the very fabric of things; beings from dimensions utterly foreign and unknown. Each is a kind unto itself.

    Running the Encounter

    Scale the incomprehensibility. Players should never fully understand what they are dealing with. Provide details that contradict, descriptions that fail, motives that don't resolve into human logic. The more they learn, the less they should know.
    Power should feel absolute. A Pseudo God cannot be defeated through direct confrontation. Combat is not a solution; though it can a method of suicide. If the players survive, it will be through cleverness, negotiation, exploitation, or simple luck.
    Benevolence does not mean safety. A Pseudo God that wishes to help may be more dangerous than the one who wishes destruction. Its gifts may be curses in disguise, and its assistance may come with prices not immediately apparent.

    Roll or choose from the following tables to generate a unique Pseudo God. The results are prompts, not prescriptions — interpret freely and combine unexpectedly. Remember that contradiction is not a flaw but a feature. These things should not make sense.
